# A small offer noordclick can deliver well

The owner asked us to decide the pricing and avoid complicated websites. These are the implemented decisions, replacing the earlier five-tier CRM catalogue. EUR remains the currency because the existing sales context targets small hospitality businesses in the Netherlands; Icelandic company identity does not by itself determine customer currency or VAT treatment.

## The two builds

| Build | Price, excluding applicable VAT | Scope |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| One good page | €950 once | One static page with up to six sections |
| A little more room | €1,650 once | Up to five static pages, one consistent design |

Both start from an established layout adapted to the customer's branding, approved text and photographs. Include mobile layouts, accessible basic navigation, page titles/descriptions, contact and map links, a link to an existing booking service, two grouped revision rounds, launch help on one agreed static-compatible host, and the website files. A menu PDF or up to six short menu/service/room entries is included.

One page is the default recommendation. Use five pages only when the business benefits from separate services, menu, story or gallery pages. All five showroom demos are accurately priced as one-page starting points. The small-site package requires actual additional pages when sold; the one-page demo is not a five-page delivery.

Typical timing is 1–2 weeks for one page and 2–3 weeks for a small site after approved content is ready. Confirm the calendar in the written quote.

## Four extras, with hard limits

| Extra | Price | Limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Help with the words | €250 | Shape supplied notes into up to 1,000 words total; one copy edit round |
| Your menu, put online | €100 | Enter a longer list, up to 30 supplied items; no ordering or menu management tool |
| One more language | €350 | Mirror the agreed pages in one second language; customer supplies translations |
| Booking widget | €150 | Embed one existing provider's supported widget; no API work; provider account and fees belong to customer |

The second language may double the physical page count, but it is a mirror of the original scope, not five additional designs. Copywriting is based on customer notes; research, legal drafting and translation are not promised. If a booking provider needs custom integration, use its simple link or decline that extra. Never charge for a widget when only an included link is delivered.

## What to decline

Online shops and on-site payments; logins, memberships and customer accounts; custom booking systems and availability logic; CMS/editor dashboards; apps; custom APIs and integrations; continuous content entry. Do not create an open-ended custom tier as a workaround. Those projects need a different supplier or a future explicit business decision.

After launch, quote each new request before doing it. Do not sell a care plan, promise unlimited edits or imply an emergency support SLA. A defect against the original agreement must be assessed under that agreement, not automatically billed as new work. Quote and project terms must address correction obligations.

## Why these prices

€950 leaves room for a proper conversation, design adaptation, two feedback rounds, responsive checking and launch. €1,650 adds a useful €700 allowance for up to four additional pages without turning the sale into a custom software project. Four extras cover the common small-business needs without charging separately for ordinary links or basic metadata.

For internal planning, aim for roughly 8–12 delivery hours on a one-page build and 16–22 on a five-page build. These are starting effort budgets, not measured margins or promised profit; track real time on the first projects and revise the business offer if the work consistently exceeds them. Sales, tax, overhead and unpaid support still consume revenue.

A limited 25 September 2026 sense-check used advertised pricing at [WebArctic](https://webarctic.nl/) and [DirectWebsite](https://directwebsite.nl/). Their scopes and recurring arrangements differ, so their prices are not treated as a market average or a like-for-like comparison. noordclick's figures are its own scope-based business decision.

## Written quote boundaries

List the actual pages/sections, supplied content, selected extras, revision rounds, provider costs, launch destination, delivery estimate, payment schedule and usage rights. Agree them before work starts. The site does not invent deposits, cancellation terms or VAT rules. Domain, hosting and provider accounts stay in the customer's name, with recurring fees paid separately to those providers.
