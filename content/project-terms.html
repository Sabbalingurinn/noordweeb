These standard terms are for new business website projects. A written quote identifies the exact scope, price, applicable tax, timing and any agreed exception. The final quote must be accepted in writing before work starts. A prepared website brief or price request is not an order.

What we build

One good page is €950 for one static page with up to six sections. A little more room is €1,650 for up to five static pages. Optional extras are copy help (€250), longer menu entry (€100), one more language (€350) and one supported booking widget (€150). Prices exclude applicable VAT. Your quote confirms the pages, extras and delivery plan. You provide approved words, images, translations, business details and third-party accounts unless the quote says otherwise.

Quote and payment

A written quote is valid for 30 calendar days from its issue date. Once you accept it in writing, we invoice 50% of the project price. The other 50% is invoiced at handover. Each invoice is due within 14 calendar days. Work starts after we receive the first payment and the content and access needed to begin. Applicable tax is confirmed on the final quote and invoice.

Changes and corrections

Each package includes two consolidated correction rounds within the agreed scope. Send each round as one clear set of comments. A new feature, page or major change of direction is priced separately before work starts. If the delivered work fails to meet the written scope, tell us promptly. We prioritise reports made within 30 calendar days of delivery, assess them within five business days and fix confirmed defects at no extra charge within a reasonable schedule agreed with you. The 30-day priority period does not remove statutory rights.

If you cancel

You can cancel in writing. Before work begins, we refund the initial payment except for non-refundable third-party costs you approved in advance. After work begins, you pay for documented work already completed, capped at the agreed project price, plus previously approved third-party costs. We credit anything already paid and refund any excess. There is no separate cancellation penalty.

Handover and rights

Your domain, hosting, email and booking-provider accounts stay in your name; their fees are separate and approved before purchase. After full payment, we hand over the agreed website files and necessary access. You receive a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free right to use, host and modify the bespoke website delivered for your business. Your supplied content remains yours. Our pre-existing tools and reusable components remain ours; third-party images, fonts and services keep their own licence terms. There is no required maintenance subscription. Later changes are quoted separately.

What is outside this offer

Simple contact, map and existing booking links, a menu PDF or up to six short menu/service entries, search basics and help launching on one agreed suitable host are included. Online shops, on-site payments, customer accounts, custom booking systems, CMS dashboards and custom integrations are outside the fixed packages. The showroom businesses and their menus, rooms and prices are fictional examples, not past client projects.
