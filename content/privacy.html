Last updated 1 October 2026. This page explains the current NoordWeeb website and our handling of enquiries and business leads. The site is still a private preview; the privacy contact below can be used now.

Who is responsible?

Tæknistoð ehf. in Iceland is the controller for NoordWeeb's enquiries and sales records. Sævar Breki Snorrason and Emil Daniel Welling handle them for the studio. For a privacy question or request, write to noordweeb@noordweeb.nl.

What this website receives

The price-request form runs in your browser. The name, business name, email address and message you type are used to prepare a price request or email draft on your device. The site does not submit those fields to a server or store them in browser storage. A downloaded request stays on your device; copying it uses your clipboard. If you choose to email us, your email provider sends it and we receive the message. Nothing is sent automatically.

We do not place analytics, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Images and fonts are served with the site. Our hosting provider may receive technical request information such as an IP address, request time and browser data to serve and secure the site. We will confirm the actual hosting provider and its log-retention arrangements before public launch.

How we use enquiries

When you email us, we use your contact details, business details, message and subsequent correspondence to reply, prepare a quote, arrange and deliver work, and keep a record of what was agreed. Depending on the stage, the legal basis is steps requested before a contract or performance of a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in responding to and managing a business enquiry (Article 6(1)(f)). Required invoice information may be retained separately to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). We do not sell enquiry data or add enquirers to a newsletter.

Business leads in our CRM

We may record limited business contact details from publicly available business websites, OpenStreetMap or Google Places, together with website observations and our conversation notes, to assess whether our small-business website service is relevant. A listing does not give us permission to send promotional email. We only send electronic marketing if a valid permission or applicable existing-customer exception has been established under the rules that apply to the recipient. If we contact you using details obtained elsewhere, we identify the source and provide this notice at the first permitted contact. We use a legitimate-interest basis for limited lead assessment (Article 6(1)(f)); you can object to direct marketing at any time without giving a reason.

The CRM can prepare outreach text but does not send messages. Optional AI drafting with xAI must remain off in public operation until the studio has checked its processing and international-transfer terms and updated this notice for the actual arrangement. We do not use automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people.

Who may receive data

Our email, website hosting and CRM/database providers process data to deliver their services; an agreed website or booking provider may receive project details needed for the customer's site. We share only what is needed. Provider names, processing locations and any transfer safeguards must be confirmed for the production setup before public launch. Email sent by you is also processed by your own email provider.

How long we keep it

  • Unconverted enquiries: 12 months after the last meaningful two-way exchange.
  • Public-source leads with no reply or project: 6 months after collection or last verified update. A lead marked “Not a fit” is removed after 30 days.
  • Won-project quotes, briefs and delivery notes: 24 months after handover, unless a live dispute or legal duty requires longer.
  • Accounting and invoice records: the applicable statutory period, held separately from the sales CRM.

If you object to marketing, we stop immediately and retain only the minimum suppression information needed to avoid contacting you again. The owners review records monthly and delete or anonymise expired material from the CRM, mailbox and exports. This is a manual process, not automatic deletion. Backup copies follow the provider's expiry cycle and are not used for active sales.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, restrict its use, object to processing, or request portability where applicable. Email us at noordweeb@noordweeb.nl; we may verify your identity proportionately. You can also complain to the data-protection authority in your country or to Iceland's Persónuvernd. We will update this notice if we add a server-side enquiry form, analytics, payments or new processors.
